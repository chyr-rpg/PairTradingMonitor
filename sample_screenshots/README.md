# PairMonitor Alert Examples

This folder contains example screenshots of **PairMonitor Telegram alerts and pair-monitoring outputs**.

The examples show how PairMonitor presents relative-value signals across US stocks and ETFs, including:

- pair charts and recent price behaviour,
- Residual-spread signals,
- relative high / low conditions,
- relative-momentum conditions,
- signal quality scores,
- previous signal context,
- pair return changes,
- and follow-up monitoring information.

Examples currently include pairs such as:

- TSLA vs QQQ
- APP vs SPY
- SPY vs QQQ
- MSFT vs PLTR

## What an Alert Represents

A PairMonitor alert does **not** represent an automatic buy or sell recommendation.

It indicates that the relationship between two instruments has reached a condition identified by the system's statistical models and may warrant further research.

For example, the system may identify that one instrument is trading relatively high or low versus a comparison instrument, or that relative momentum is strengthening or weakening.
